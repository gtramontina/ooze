# Cross-platform acceptance gate: platform facts and evidence obligations

_Research date: 2026-08-25. Audit input for [Set the cross-platform acceptance gate](https://github.com/gtramontina/ooze/issues/65). This note does not take ownership of #68, #69, or #70. The later policy choices are recorded in [the acceptance gate](../acceptance-gate.md)._

## Conclusion

The permanent gate must keep four kinds of evidence distinct:

1. pure unit/simulation evidence, which can run on every host and must replay the production reducers;
2. native platform integration evidence, which must execute on Linux, Darwin, and Windows because cross-compilation cannot validate kernel behavior;
3. deliberate mutation evidence, in which each absence-oriented native fixture is observed failing against the specific broken instrument it is meant to detect; and
4. performance/stress evidence, which records distributions and resource behavior separately from correctness and never turns a noisy timing observation into a waiver.

An unsupported target is a compile-and-construction contract, not a fourth native implementation: the accepted #61 contract requires construction to fail before admission or launch. Conversely, a supported platform that was not executed is **inconclusive**, not green. The inherited `TestSupervisorDriverReadyEarlierRootExitBeatsDeadlineAndSamples` timeout at the signed #67 tip is an inherited regression recorded by #66; excluding it can isolate fixture evidence, but cannot satisfy the full landing gate or convert the failure into acceptable evidence ([#66 delivery record](https://github.com/gtramontina/ooze/issues/66#issuecomment-5396266069), [#67 acceptance record](https://github.com/gtramontina/ooze/issues/67#issuecomment-5392663020)).

## Linux: subreaper adoption and depth-one accounting

`PR_SET_CHILD_SUBREAPER` reparents an orphan to its nearest still-living ancestor subreaper. That subreaper becomes the orphan's parent, receives `SIGCHLD`, and can wait for its termination. The attribute is Linux-specific, is preserved across `execve`, and is not inherited across `fork` or `clone` ([Linux `PR_SET_CHILD_SUBREAPER`](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/PR_SET_CHILD_SUBREAPER.2const.html)). `wait4`/`waitpid` select children, not arbitrary descendants; therefore the accepted repeated depth-one sweep is essential: adopted or newly revealed children become waitable only through the subreaper relationship ([Linux `wait4`](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/wait4.2.html)).

The Linux matrix cannot translate Darwin's “visible PID” columns mechanically. Its native oracle answers whether the guardian currently has a child that can be waited for. The gate therefore has to construct each subject shape with positive identity evidence, show its expected parent/adoption relation at both root states, and show that repeated wait/sweep rounds reach `ECHILD` only after every constructed subject is reaped. PID identity can be strengthened with `/proc/<pid>/stat` field 22 (`starttime`), which is the process start time since boot ([Linux `/proc/pid/stat`](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man5/proc_pid_stat.5.html)).

Linux's `/proc/<pid>/task/<tid>/children` is only a first-level view and the kernel documentation warns that children can be skipped; it recommends stopping the inspected processes when precise results are required. That makes it corroborating shape evidence, not a standalone authoritative empty result, and supports the accepted freeze/fixpoint discipline where a process-table walk is used ([Linux procfs children documentation](https://www.kernel.org/doc/html/latest/filesystems/proc.html#proc-pid-task-tid-children-information-about-task-children)).

Required Linux shapes are at least plain child, deeper chain, double-fork/session or process-group escape, continuous/mid-drain forking, and an adopted orphan after root exit. A deliberate mutation must conflate the actual subreaper/wait oracle with a weaker root walk or terminate after a single sweep and make the exact affected rows red. A compile-only Linux build does not exercise `prctl`, reparenting, `SIGCHLD`, or `wait4`, so it is supplemental evidence only.

## Darwin: incomplete instruments and limits that must stay visible

Darwin has no accepted subreaper primitive in this contract. The repository's live-root parent walk, group census, and walk seeded from live group members are individually incomplete, which is why the matrix must retain separate columns and both root states rather than reduce to one “drained” boolean ([live #65 worked matrix](https://github.com/gtramontina/ooze/issues/65), [existing observability research](runaway-fuse-observability.md)). Apple's XNU header explicitly says `NOTE_TRACK`, `NOTE_TRACKERR`, and `NOTE_CHILD` have been unsupported since 10.5, so they cannot close the descendant-tracking gap ([Apple XNU `event.h`](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/main/bsd/sys/event.h)).

The native acceptance fixture must prove this ordering, not merely its final outcome: capture live group members and their parent-identity closure; stop/freeze the group and captured escapees; repeat enumerate-and-freeze until the identity set stabilizes under one absolute bound; only then kill by group and birth-validated identity. It must cover generation/action correlation, identity mismatch or unreadability, and PID reuse without ever signalling a mismatched identity. The behind-live-member escape must be green. Direct-root/pre-freeze escape, mid-fork escape, supervisor death, and Darwin's remaining check-to-signal PID-reuse window stay explicit limits; observing one is a successful limit fixture, not proof of containment ([#61 resolution](https://github.com/gtramontina/ooze/issues/61#issuecomment-5386039287), [#66 direct/reachable fixture allocation](https://github.com/gtramontina/ooze/issues/66#issuecomment-5384261351)).

## Windows: Job containment, breakaway, nesting, and accounting

By default, a process assigned to a Job has children associated with that Job. Explicit and silent breakaway are enabled only by `JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_BREAKAWAY_OK` and `JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_SILENT_BREAKAWAY_OK`; with neither limit set, a child cannot escape merely by passing `CREATE_BREAKAWAY_FROM_JOB` ([Microsoft Job Objects](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/procthread/job-objects)). Nested Jobs have existed since Windows 8/Server 2012. A child process is associated with every Job in its parent's chain, parent Job accounting aggregates child Jobs, and terminating a Job terminates processes in its descendant Jobs ([Microsoft Nested Jobs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/procthread/nested-jobs)).

Several Darwin rows therefore collapse on Windows: ordinary descendant depth and root exit do not change transitive Job membership. The interesting matrix rows are ordinary child/deep descendant, denied explicit breakaway, and a runnable descendant placed in a nested Job, each observed before and after root exit through Job membership/accounting plus exact retained process-handle evidence where the fixture needs to distinguish a running process from stale accounting. The gate must also prove `JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_KILL_ON_JOB_CLOSE` behavior and handle lifetime rather than treating `CloseHandle` itself as an emptiness observation ([Microsoft Job Objects](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/procthread/job-objects)).

Job counters must be interpreted exactly. `TotalProcesses` is lifetime association attempts, `ActiveProcesses` is current membership, and `TotalTerminatedProcesses` counts limit-violation terminations rather than every exit or kill. An active count can remain elevated until all references to a terminated process are released ([Microsoft `JOBOBJECT_BASIC_ACCOUNTING_INFORMATION`](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winnt/ns-winnt-jobobject_basic_accounting_information)). Completion-port notifications are not generally guaranteed, so `ACTIVE_PROCESS_ZERO` or an exit message may support a bounded observation but cannot replace a confirming Job query and retained subject handle ([Microsoft Job completion ports](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winnt/ns-winnt-jobobject_associate_completion_port)).

Windows native execution is mandatory for these rows. The #66 history found an actual Windows-only assertion regression after all cross-compiles had passed, which directly demonstrates that compilation is not runtime evidence ([#66 three-platform measurement](https://github.com/gtramontina/ooze/issues/66#issuecomment-5384435755)). The Go race detector supports `windows/amd64`, but not Windows ARM64, so Windows ARM64 can be cross-compiled while the race-enabled native lane must use AMD64 ([Go race-detector requirements](https://go.dev/doc/articles/race_detector)).

## Simulation, replay, shrinking, and conformance

The landing gate must exercise #64's accepted A/G3 contract through the real production transition seams. Ordinary exploration chooses only legal enabled facts; malformed stale, duplicate, contradictory, wrong-token, and wrong-generation facts belong to `ReplayViolation`, which must deterministically clean up and re-panic. Replay compares exact owner state/effects at every record and the composed world at quiescent barriers/terminal settlement. Semantic shrinking removes records and definition members while preserving the typed failure key; a byte-level Go fuzz minimizer is not a substitute ([#64 resolution](https://github.com/gtramontina/ooze/issues/64#issuecomment-5395720358)).

Go's fuzzing documentation reinforces the allocation: fuzz targets should be fast, deterministic, and independent across calls; discovered failures are minimized and written to the corpus, and promoted seeds run under ordinary `go test`. Coverage-guided fuzzing should execute on supported AMD64/ARM64 hosts, and time-bounded CI must set `-fuzztime` because the default run is otherwise unbounded ([Go fuzzing](https://go.dev/doc/security/fuzz/)). For #65 this supports a bounded discovery lane plus deterministic promoted traces in the required unit lane; it does not require a durable trace codec, which #64 explicitly deferred.

Focused simulation evidence must cover all #64/#61 named boundaries: before/equality/after deadlines; stale and duplicate facts; start versus closure; late grants and release revocation/adoption; multiple provisionals and FIFO barriers; full-to-single admission; terminal versus fatal commitment; empty and non-empty emergency settlement; mixed invariant/containment dominance; output cutoff; and exact ordered prospective/owned residuals. Production/simulation conformance must fake only native effects and clocks, never the reducer or shell transition authority.

## Unit, integration, mutation, stress, and performance gate shape

### Required on every pull request

- Pure reducer, driver, simulation replay/violation/shrink/conformance tests under `-race`, coverage, and shuffle on the repository's primary Linux job.
- The same normal race/coverage/shuffle suite on native Darwin and native Windows AMD64, so supported-platform adapter behavior executes rather than merely compiles.
- One bounded pass of each green native platform-contract fixture, including drain expiry and per-shape/per-instrument/root-state matrices. A skip for a required native instrument is inconclusive and fails the required job unless it is the fixture for a documented platform limit.
- Cross-compilation for Windows AMD64/ARM64, Linux AMD64 (and maintained native architecture), and the unsupported sentinel target; the sentinel must compile and reject construction before native launch. Cross-compilation supplements but never replaces native lanes.
- A deliberate-mutation evidence check tied to every new absence-oriented instrument fixture. It may be an isolated workflow/target rather than mutating the checked-in source, but the artifact must name the mutation, expected red rows, and observed failure. The #66 Windows mutation runs are reusable historical proof for those unchanged fixtures; a fixture or instrument change invalidates that proof.

Go documents `-count` as repeated execution and `-shuffle` as randomized test/benchmark order with a reported reproducible seed; neither changes the truth criteria of a fixture ([Go test flags](https://go.dev/cmd/go/?m=old)). The race detector adds substantial runtime and memory overhead, so race timing must not be compared directly with non-race performance baselines ([Go race-detector overhead](https://go.dev/doc/articles/race_detector)).

The repository workflow audit finds a tooling gap as well as an evidence gap: `.github/workflows/ci.yml` installs Devbox and uses it for Go-backed targets, while `os-compatibility.yml`, `mutation.yml`, and `compatibility.yml` currently install or invoke Go directly. None invokes `test.adversarial` or `test.adversarial.stress`. #65's workflow changes must preserve the pinned toolchain while routing every Go command through Devbox as required by the delivery discipline ([CI workflow](../../.github/workflows/ci.yml), [mutation workflow](../../.github/workflows/mutation.yml), [compatibility workflow](../../.github/workflows/compatibility.yml)).

### Stress and performance lanes

Run the existing independently bounded `test.adversarial.stress` on all three native supported platforms at least on main/nightly or before the landing decision, preserving the exact test list and per-fixture watchdogs. Stress failure is correctness evidence; stress success is confidence evidence, not a proof that an untested shape is safe.

Performance evidence should compare end-to-end healthy campaign wall time, attempt throughput, peak process count, and memory on Linux, Darwin, and Windows for a fast healthy campaign, process-heavy command, provisional-confirmation wave, cleanup escalation, and concurrent campaigns. It must also assert structural invariants that do not depend on host speed: no extra healthy-path attempt, no accidental serialization below accepted admission, one bounded concurrent drain epoch, and no quadratic control-plane scan where owner-local indexing is available ([repository performance guidance](tigerstyle-for-ooze.md)). Go benchmarks report wall-clock `ns/op`; parallel benchmark results are wall time for the benchmark as a whole, not summed CPU time, so benchmark artifacts must include platform, architecture, Go version, race mode, count, and sample distribution before comparing them ([Go `testing.B.RunParallel`](https://pkg.go.dev/testing#B.RunParallel)).

For automated A/B benchmark comparison, Go's official `benchstat` documentation recommends identical configurations, interleaved before/after runs, at least ten samples (ideally twenty), and an idle, non-throttled machine; its statistical significance output does not decide whether a change is materially important ([`benchstat` documentation](https://pkg.go.dev/golang.org/x/perf/cmd/benchstat)). GitHub-hosted runner hardware and images vary by operating system and are updated over time, so results must stay separated by runner/OS/architecture and remain observational unless #65 binds a stable comparator and material threshold ([GitHub-hosted runners](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/runners/github-hosted-runners)).

No accepted source supplies a universal percentage regression threshold. A performance observation without a predeclared comparator and threshold is descriptive or inconclusive, not authority to fail or waive correctness. Structural regressions are blockers; noisy wall-time deltas require a recorded baseline/distribution and an explicit #65 policy before becoming a required check.

## Binding policy gaps for #65 to close

1. **Performance threshold:** the scenarios and measurements are already named, but no binding maximum slowdown or statistical rule exists. #65 must either choose one with measured baselines or explicitly make performance artifacts reviewed landing evidence rather than an automated percentage check.
2. **Stress cadence:** #66 supplied a ten-repeat target but deliberately left whether it gates each PR, main, nightly, or only the landing commit to #65.
3. **Mutation proof retention:** #66 has authoritative red-run artifacts for its Windows fixtures, but policy must say when code or fixture changes require regenerating those artifacts. The defensible boundary is any change to the fixture, subject construction, instrument, or production path it exercises.
4. **Inherited timing regression:** no issue amendment authorizes waiving `TestSupervisorDriverReadyEarlierRootExitBeatsDeadlineAndSamples`. Until it passes or a binding owner explicitly reclassifies it, the complete landing gate remains red even when a focused fixture run excludes it.
5. **Required native architecture:** contracts are per operating system, while race support differs by architecture. The minimal supported live set is Linux (repository runner architecture), Darwin ARM64/AMD64 as available, and Windows AMD64; additional maintained architectures need cross-compile evidence unless project policy names them as live-supported.
6. **Inconclusive classification:** unavailable or unreadable identity, unstable process enumeration, Job-query/handle failure, and bounded fixpoint expiry must remain visible. The accepted drainage contract already says these cannot prove emptiness, but #65 still has to bind whether each required-lane observation fails immediately or produces a separately reviewed inconclusive artifact; it cannot silently pass or skip.
