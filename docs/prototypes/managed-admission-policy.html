Superseded exploration — not the selected design

This prototype preserves the rejected min(P, 2) ramp-and-halve policy for design history. The selected policy starts automatic admission at fixed P and, only after validated evidence that Ooze-owned overlap contributed to pressure, irreversibly reduces automatic admission to 1 for the process runtime. It has no ramp, proportional backoff, or recovery probe.

How cautiously should Ooze trust available capacity?

This demo explores one candidate admission policy for automatic campaigns. It deliberately models only Ooze’s process-local admission decisions—not operating-system containment, deadlines, scoring, or queue mechanics.

Question under test: after the mandatory clean automatic baseline supplies the singleton observation, should shared admission start at min(P, 2), grow by one only after a clean drained primary proves the current limit was fully used, and—after confirmed contention—halve once and stop growing for the life of this process runtime?
